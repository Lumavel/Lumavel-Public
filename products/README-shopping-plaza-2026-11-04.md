# Lumavel Shopping Plaza

Updated **2026-11-04**.

A community-centered marketplace for independent creators and small businesses. Browse partner shops and listings. Approved sellers manage a storefront, listings, and orders. Shop applications are reviewed by people. The Plaza is in early access and is part of the core Lumavel website.
