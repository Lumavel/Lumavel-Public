## What it is

Informational wellness app (core-lee-rah). **Full browser app** on lumavel.co: Today, Track (food, hydration, movement, sleep, body, symptoms, reproductive/PCOS, medications), Progress, You, Quick Log, onboarding. Not medical care. Data on this device for now (Clerk + localStorage). Expo remains for a later Play/iOS wrap.
