# Lumavel

**Software, games, and learning.**

Lumavel builds games, software, and practical tools for everyday life. Play together, create a QR code, discover an independent shop, or publish a page for your links.

[Explore Lumavel](https://lumavel.co) · [Products](https://lumavel.co/products) · [About](https://lumavel.co/about) · [Contact](https://lumavel.co/contact)

## Shopping Plaza

The Plaza is part of the main Lumavel website. Discover partner shops and products, learn who runs a shop, and explore listings. Shop applications are reviewed by people.

- [Browse shops and listings](https://lumavel.co/lumavelshoppingplaza)
- [Our Plaza](https://lumavel.co/lumavelshoppingplaza/our-plaza)
- [Apply to open a shop](https://lumavel.co/lumavelshoppingplaza/apply)

## Lumavel Pages

Create a public pay page or social page. Add payment destinations, social profiles, and other links. Visitors choose the destination they want to open; payments are handled there.

[Explore Lumavel Pages](https://lumavel.co/info/lumavel-pages).
